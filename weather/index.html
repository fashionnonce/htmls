Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Introduction

Weather Life (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides weather forecasts and lifestyle suggestions. This policy explains how the app and its service providers process information when you use the app. For privacy questions or requests, contact fashionnonce@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

The app does not create user accounts and does not request Android device-location permission. It uses the following information to provide its features:

  • City and weather data: The selected city and its coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve forecasts. City search terms are sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service.
  • Local app data: The selected city and cached forecasts are stored on your device to restore the app and reduce repeat requests. Weather Life does not upload this local cache to its own server.
  • Network information: Network providers, Open-Meteo, and Google Mobile Ads may receive your IP address and standard request/device information when their services are used.
  • Advertising data: Google Mobile Ads may process device and advertising identifiers (where available and permitted), app/device information, IP-derived general location, ad requests, interactions, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Ad personalization is subject to your consent choices and applicable settings.
  • Consent choices: Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) stores and uses advertising consent and privacy choices to apply the selected ad settings.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide accurate weather forecasts and lifestyle advice.
  • Retrieve weather forecasts and resolve city searches.
  • Display and measure ads, apply privacy choices, and prevent invalid activity.
  • Store the selected city and recent forecast locally on your device.

Data Sharing

Weather Life does not operate an account or weather-data backend. Data is sent to these providers when their features are used:

  • Open-Meteo: receives selected city coordinates for forecasts and search terms for geocoding.
  • Google Mobile Ads: receives ad requests and related information to provide ads and measurement according to your consent and its policies.
  • Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect safety and rights.

Third-Party Services

Third-party providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. See Open-Meteo's terms, Google's Privacy Policy, and AdMob policies.

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no system is completely secure.

Your Choices

The app does not infer your city from your IP address. You can choose a city manually in the app. You can change the selected city at any time. To remove the locally stored city and forecast, clear the app's storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. For questions or requests about information handled by a provider, contact that provider directly using its privacy resources.

Data Retention

The selected city and cached forecast remain on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. Third-party providers determine their own retention periods under their published policies.

Children

Weather Life is not designed for or directed to children. The app's ads and data practices are configured for the declared adult/general audience.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will revise the effective date above.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email fashionnonce@gmail.com.
