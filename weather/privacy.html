WeatherBloom Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Information We Process

WeatherBloom does not access device GPS or other system location services and does not request location permission. A preset city is shown on first use; you can search for and select a city. Search terms are sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service, and the selected city's coordinates are sent to the Open-Meteo weather service to retrieve forecasts.

Third-Party Services and Network Information

When these online services receive a request, they receive the IP address and connection information needed to provide the service. The app does not send your IP address to an IP-geolocation service. The app uses Google Fonts; fonts may be downloaded from Google. The Google Mobile Ads SDK remains an app dependency, but the current iOS build does not initialize it or request ads by default. Other platforms, or builds that explicitly enable ads, may use that service. Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your selected city and weather cache locally to remember your city and support short-term offline viewing. Weather data is considered current for 30 minutes; local data may remain until overwritten or the app is uninstalled. The app does not require an account and has no developer-operated user-profile server. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.

Tracking and Analytics

The current iOS build does not initialize the advertising SDK or request ads by default. The app does not separately integrate an analytics or crash-reporting service. Weather-based suggestions are generated on your device.

Weather and Lifestyle Suggestions

Forecasts and lifestyle suggestions are for general information only and are not medical, health, or emergency advice. Conditions can change; check local official alerts and use your judgment for health or safety decisions.
